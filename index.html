01 / About

What is HARDWIRED?

HARDWIRED is a state-level 36-hour hackathon hosted by the ISTE Student Chapter MEC at Marian Engineering College, Thiruvananthapuram.

Compete against the sharpest minds across Kerala. Build real solutions to real problems. No fluff, no filler — just you, your team, and 36 hours on the clock.

Push your limits. Break the mold. Get hardwired.
