01 / About

What is HARDWIRED?

HARDWIRED is a 30-hour inter-college hardware hackathon organized by the ISTE Student Chapter at Marian Engineering College, Trivandrum bringing together ambitious students, innovators, and future industry leaders from across various institutions.

Focused on hardware innovation, embedded systems, IoT, and robotics, the event bridges the gap between academic learning and practical technological development.

A platform where creativity meets engineering excellence. Push your limits. Break the mold. Get hardwired.

Event Schedule

Day 1 — 8:00 AM – 8:45 AM
Registration & Check-in
Arrive at the venue, verify your team details, and grab your hackathon kits before we begin.
Day 1 — 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM
Inauguration Ceremony
Official kickoff, rules briefing, and introduction to the tracks and judging panel.
Day 1 — 10:00 AM
Hack Begins — 30 Hours Start
The clock starts ticking. Get to your workstations and start building.
Day 2 — 12:00 PM
Hackathon Ends & Submission Deadline
Wrap up your final tests, document your working prototype, and submit your project for evaluation.
Day 2 — 12:30 PM – 3:30 PM

Day 2 — 4:30 PM – 5:00 PM
Winners Announcement
Prize distribution, closing remarks, and celebrating the top hardware innovations.
